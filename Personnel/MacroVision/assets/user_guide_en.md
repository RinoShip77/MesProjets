# 📖 User Guide

Welcome to **MacroVision**! Here is how to get the best results from your AI scanner.

### 📸 1. Meal Analysis (Photo)
Take a photo of your plate to estimate calories.
* **Tip:** Ensure good lighting.
* **Tip:** Separate food items so the AI can distinguish them (e.g., keep the steak separate from the fries).
* **Note:** The AI estimates portion sizes based on the plate size.

### 🏷️ 2. Nutrition Label Scan
Scan the table on the back of a package.
* **Tip:** Flatten the wrapper to remove creases.
* **Tip:** Avoid glare from overhead lights on shiny plastic.
* **Result:** The app will extract the exact values for you.

### ║▌ 3. Barcode Scanner
Scan the product's barcode for quick lookup.
* **Usage:** Center the red line over the barcode.
* **Database:** We use OpenFoodFacts. If a product is unknown, try the **Label Scan** instead.

### ✏️ Editing Entries
AI isn't perfect! You can always tap on any nutritional value (Calories, Protein, etc.) on the result screen to correct it manually before saving.
